Key System Features

Designed for fast, reliable, and educational traffic sign recognition with complete road safety guidance.

Fast Detection

Rapid computer vision algorithms process uploaded road photos in fractions of a second, isolating sign candidates with high efficiency.

Traffic Sign Recognition

Accurately identifies Stop signs, Speed limits, No Entry markers, Turn arrows, Yield signs, and Pedestrian crossings using color and shape analysis.

Clear Sign Meaning

Provides clear, plain-language legal definitions for each recognized traffic sign so drivers and learners understand the exact road rule.

Driver Action

Specifies actionable driving responses—such as stopping completely, reducing speed, checking mirrors, or yielding precedence.

Detection History

Every completed scan is stored in a relational MySQL database, providing an organized audit log to review past detection outcomes anytime.

Processing Time

Dynamically benchmarks and displays the exact elapsed processing duration in seconds, offering full visibility into computational speed.

How The System Works

A 4-step streamlined image processing and detection pipeline.

1

Upload Image

User selects a road or traffic photograph in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format with instant preview.

2

Preprocess Image

Python and OpenCV convert color space to HSV, apply Gaussian blur, and filter noise.

3

Detect Traffic Sign

Contour detection evaluates geometry (circularity, polygons) and classifies the sign candidate.

4

Display Meaning & Action

Outputs the annotated image, legal meaning, driver instruction, and execution time in seconds.
